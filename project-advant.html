It Deserved.

Advant Corporate is a corporate governance consulting and advisory firm. They support both individuals and corporate clients—ranging from small businesses to large organizations in navigating governance-related challenges. But their brand — their visual identity, their messaging, their online presence — was non-existent.

E-Vast came in to fix that from the ground up. We built a complete brand foundation: positioning strategy, visual brand identity, messaging architecture, and a social media execution plan that made Advant unmistakably distinct in a crowded legal startup market.

The result was 100% brand clarity — every prospect now immediately understood who Advant was, what they stood for, and why they were the firm to call. Social media grew by 80% as the new brand voice attracted the right audience.

The Brief
01
The Challenge

A credible corporate firm with no brand strategy or execution in place. Their expertise was real but their presence didn't exist — they were invisible in a market where perception is everything and trust must be established before a client ever makes contact.

02
Our Approach

We started with brand strategy — defining Advant's positioning, their ideal client profile, and what made them genuinely different. From there we built a visual identity system and messaging architecture that expressed that difference clearly. Social media execution brought it all to life consistently.

03
The Outcome

100% brand clarity — prospects immediately understood Advant's positioning and offer. 80% social media growth as the new brand voice attracted a more aligned audience. A legal firm that now looks and sounds like the market leader it is.
