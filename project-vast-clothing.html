Overview

From Drop to
Sold Out in
18 Days.

Vast Clothing approached E-Vast with a limited edition capsule collection they needed to sell out — fast. The challenge wasn't the product; it was attention. In a saturated Nigerian fashion market, a limited drop means nothing without the right narrative and the right amplification system behind it.

We built a campaign around scarcity, cultural relevance, and model-led social proof. In under 48 hours we generated 5M impressions. Within 18 days, every single limited edition piece was sold out. Instagram grew by 120% during the campaign window.

The Brief
01
The Challenge

Launch a limited edition fashion drop in a crowded market. Create genuine demand, not just visibility. Convert attention into purchases — fast — before the cultural moment passed.

02
Our Approach

A narrative-first campaign built around scarcity and identity. Strategic influencer seeding to the right voices — not the biggest accounts, but the most trusted ones in the target audience. Paid amplification timed to the peak of organic momentum.

03
The Outcome

5M impressions in under 48 hours. Sold out in 18 days. 3× ROAS on paid media. 120% Instagram growth. The campaign became one of the most talked-about fashion drops in its category in the first week.
