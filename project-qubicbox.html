Qubicbox is a cybersecurity company with serious technical capability — but no brand presence to match it. In a field where trust and credibility are everything, being invisible online is a commercial liability.

E-Vast was brought in to build Qubicbox's foundational digital presence from scratch — establishing them as a credible, knowledgeable authority in the Nigerian cybersecurity space. We did this through educational content, strategic LinkedIn positioning, and relatable tech creative that made a niche industry accessible.

The result: zero-to-viral in 72 hours, 3M impressions, and a 200% LinkedIn growth that put Qubicbox on the radar of exactly the right audience.

The Brief
01
The Challenge

A technically excellent company with no online voice. Zero brand content. No LinkedIn presence. In cybersecurity, where clients need to trust you before they hire you, this invisibility was costing them real business.

02
Our Approach

We built a content architecture around three pillars: education (making cybersecurity threats accessible and understandable), credibility (positioning the team as genuine experts), and relatability (tech memes and culturally-relevant content that drove organic sharing without compromising authority).

03
The Outcome

3M impressions. 200% LinkedIn growth. A tech meme post that got 3.5K views and drove a wave of new followers from exactly the right audience. Qubicbox went from invisible to recognized in their category within weeks.
