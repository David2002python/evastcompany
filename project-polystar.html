Overview

Building

Revenue Machine.

POLYSTAR is a brand name in electronics and home appliances sub-sector of the Nigerian market. Polystar Electronics Ogba was a well-known physical branch at Aguda-Ogba, Lagos, with strong walk-in traffic — but their online presence was virtually non-existent. They had no structured digital sales funnel, no SEO strategy, and no systematic approach to converting online interest into qualified buyers.

E-Vast was brought in as a full growth partner on a retainer basis. Our mandate was clear: build a digital revenue engine from the ground up — one that would generate consistent inbound leads, increase retail velocity, and ultimately make online sales a major contributor to total revenue.

Over the course of the engagement, we built, tested, and scaled a system that generated over ₦225M in revenue, 900+ qualified inbound leads, and made online sales account for 60% of total revenue.

The Brief
01
The Challenge

A thriving electronics showroom store with zero digital infrastructure. No lead generation system. No SEO presence. No paid media. No way to capture online demand or convert it into sales — while other branches and competitors were moving aggressively online.

02
Our Approach

We designed and implemented a full-stack digital growth system: SEO-optimised content, paid media campaigns on Meta and Google, a structured sales funnel with lead capture and follow-up sequences, and a social media presence that built trust with potential buyers before they even made contact.

03
The Outcome

₦225M+ in revenue generated. 900+ qualified inbound leads. Online sales grew from 0% to 60% of total revenue. Retail velocity increased by 20%. A store that once depended entirely on walk-in customers was now running a self-sustaining digital sales machine.

How It Unfolded

Phase by
Phase.

Phase 01
Audit & Foundation

Full audit of existing online presence (or lack thereof). Competitor analysis. Keyword mapping for high-intent electronics search terms in Lagos. Set up tracking, analytics, and lead capture infrastructure from scratch.

Phase 02
Funnel Build & First Campaigns

Built and launched the first Meta ad campaigns targeting in-market buyers. Simultaneously began SEO content publishing. Created WhatsApp-based follow-up sequences to convert inbound enquiries into confirmed sales.

Phase 03
Scale & Optimise

Doubled down on the ad sets generating the lowest CPL. Expanded to Google Shopping. Built out social proof content — unboxing videos, product comparison posts — to warm up cold traffic and increase conversion rates.

Phase 04
Retainer — Sustained Growth

Monthly strategy sessions. Weekly performance reporting. Continuous testing of new creative angles, offers, and audience segments. The system compound over time — each month building on the last, with online revenue growing consistently quarter over quarter.
