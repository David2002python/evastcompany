Nedfur is a furniture company specializing in the production and installation of doors, kitchen cabinets, walk in closets(wardrobes), consoles, media units, dressers, and more. They delivered quality furniture with a loyal base of existing clients. But their brand identity no longer reflected the quality of what they were selling. Their visuals seemed outdated and inconsistent with the brand itself which was costing them prospect interest and client retention.

E-Vast was brought in to relaunch Nedfur's identity — not just visually, but strategically. We rebuilt their visual brand from the ground up, redefined their messaging that made the brand feel as premium as the furniture itself.

The result was a 20% increase in client retention ratio — alongside a measurable increase in prospect interest from new buyers discovering the brand for the first time.

The Brief
01
The Challenge

A quality furniture brand with a dated, inconsistent visual identity that was actively undermining the perceived value of their products. Prospects weren't connecting to the rate of the the product quality they delivered.

02
Our Approach

We started by auditing every brand touchpoint — logo, colours, photography style, messaging. We identified the gap between the actual quality of Nedfur's product and how it was being presented. Then we rebuilt the entire identity to close that gap — making the brand feel as premium and considered as the furniture itself.

03
The Outcome

20% increase in client retention ratio. Measurable increase in prospect interest and enquiry volume. A brand that now accurately represents the quality of the product it sells — and attracts clients who can appreciate and afford that quality.
