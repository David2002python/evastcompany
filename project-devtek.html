Devtek Tanks is a water engineering company, that provides waste water/sewage management solutions, water treatment solutions, water tank storage solutions — an industry where credibility and trust determine whether you win or lose. Their technical expertise was not in question. Their brand's ability to communicate that expertise, however, was.

E-Vast was brought in to amplify Devtek's brand recognition across Africa's water and environmental management sector. We built a social media presence that communicated expertise visually — project showcases, educated content, and thought leadership that positioned Devtek as the authoritative name in their space.

The result was a 40% increase in brand credibility metrics and 70% social media growth — turning a technically excellent company into a visually compelling one.

The Brief
01
The Challenge

A highly capable water engineering company that didn't look structured online. Where reputation drives procurement decisions, their digital footprint didn't reflect the scale of their actual work.

02
Our Approach

We built a visual-first content strategy that showcased Devtek's projects, expertise, and authority. Every piece of content was designed to answer one question from a potential client: 'Can I trust these people with a significant infrastructure project?' The answer, visually, was clearly yes.

03
The Outcome

40% increase in brand credibility as measured by prospect trust and enquiry quality. 70% social media growth. Enhanced recognition across the water engineering sector in Nigeria and across Africa.
