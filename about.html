About E-Vast

Help Brands

E-Vast is a Creative PR & Marketing Agency that designs and executes high-impact campaigns that drive attention, influence perception, and convert audiences into revenue.

We are not a social media management company. We are not a content factory. We are a strategic growth partner for brands that are serious about dominance.

2020
Founded in Lagos, Nigeria
The Big Idea
"Content without
strategy is noise.
We build campaigns
that cut through."

When E-Vast was founded, the agency landscape was full of people selling posts, selling designs, selling management. Every brand was being told to "just be consistent" while the market moved faster than any posting schedule could keep up with.

We built something different. A system that engineers attention — not begs for it.

Today, E-Vast operates as a full Creative PR & Marketing agency with four specialised units working as one machine: High-Level PR, Paid Media Performance, Creative Campaigns, and Brand Growth Systems.

Our edge is our X influencer network — exclusive, curated, strategic. We don't chase virality. We engineer it.

Our Journey

The Story
So Far.

2020
Founded in Lagos

E-Vast launched as a social media agency in Lagos, Nigeria. We quickly realised the market didn't need more people posting — it needed people who could make brands impossible to ignore.

2021
First Major Campaign

Executed our first full campaign launch for a Lagos-based fintech. 3M impressions in 5 days using a narrative-first influencer strategy. The playbook that became E-Vast's signature was born here.

2022
Building the X Network

We spent the year building what became our most powerful weapon: an exclusive, curated network of X (Twitter) influencers spanning tech, finance, culture, and entertainment. Not random influencers — strategic voices.

2023
Full-Service Expansion

Evolved from a PR-first agency into a full Creative PR & Marketing partner. Added Paid Media and Brand Growth units. Revenue outcomes became measurable, not just felt.

2025
E-Vast 2.0 — The Next Level

Repositioned as a Creative PR & Marketing Partner. No longer selling posts or designs. Engineering attention. Controlling narratives. Driving revenue. The next level — for our clients and ourselves.
