E-Vast is a Creative PR & Marketing Agency that designs and executes high-impact campaigns that drive attention, influence perception, and convert audiences into revenue.

We are not a social media management company. We are not a content factory. We are a strategic growth partner for brands that are serious about dominance.

2023
Founded in Lagos, Nigeria
The Big Idea
"Content without
strategy is noise.
We build campaigns
that cut through."

When E-Vast was founded, the agency landscape was full of people selling posts, selling designs, selling management. Every brand was being told to "just be consistent" while the market moved faster than any posting schedule could keep up with.

We built something different. A system that engineers attention.

At E-Vast, we operates as a full Creative PR & Marketing agency with four specialised units working as one machine: High-Level PR, Paid Media Performance, Creative Campaigns, and Brand Growth Systems.

Our edge is our X influencer network — exclusive, curated, strategic. We don't chase virality. We engineer it.

How We Think

Our Four
Pillars.

Every service, every campaign, every decision runs through one of these four operating principles.

01
Attention
Engineering
High-Level PR Unit

We don't hope people notice our clients. We design the moment they become impossible to ignore. Through narrative control, cultural moment identification, and our exclusive influencer network, we place brands into the conversations that define markets.

02
Revenue
Conversion
Paid Media Unit

Attention without conversion is vanity. We build full-funnel paid media systems that take the visibility we create and turn it into measurable revenue — with data, discipline, and zero wasted spend.

03
Cultural
Impact
Creative Campaigns Unit

We think in campaigns, not content. Every creative brief we receive gets treated like a cultural moment waiting to happen. We develop the big idea, the narrative, the storytelling architecture that makes people remember your brand long after the campaign ends.

04
Brand
Compounding
Brand Growth Unit

Virality is a moment. Dominance is a system. We build the long-term infrastructure — positioning, consistency, and scaling systems — that turns short-term attention into lasting market authority. Brands that compound, not brands that spike.
